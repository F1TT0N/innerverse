A calm place to be

Everything in InnerVerse is made to be looked at slowly.

74 immersive scenes

Stereoscopic 360° scenes that wrap all the way around you: nebulae, auroras, rings of light, slow waves and drifting patterns, each with real depth.

25 calm tracks

Ambient, piano and guitar pieces play in a shuffled loop. Skip forward or back whenever you like.

Words of wisdom

A short quote from a philosopher, scientist or poet appears during each scene. Prefer a clear view? Turn quotes off with one switch.

Never the same twice

Scenes come in a fresh random order every session, arranged so that similar ones do not follow each other.

Your pace

Pause, change the scene or the song, and set volume and brightness from a simple menu. Your settings are remembered.

Private and offline

All scenes and music are stored on the headset. InnerVerse has no account, no ads and no internet access at all.

How it works

Sit or stand comfortably. That is all you need.

Press Start

Open InnerVerse and press the Start button. The first scene opens around you and the music begins with it.

Look around

Each scene lasts about 30 seconds and then gives way to the next one. Turn your head, or drag the scene with the joystick.

Stay as long as you like

Open the menu to pause, skip, or adjust sound and light. Press Exit when you are done.

FAQ

Which headsets does it run on?

InnerVerse is made for Meta Quest headsets and is controlled with the Touch controllers.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Every scene and every track is installed with the app, so it works anywhere, including in flight mode.

Is this a guided meditation?

No. There is no voice and no instructions, only scenes, music and an optional quote. Use it to unwind, to breathe, or as a calm background for your own practice.

Can I turn the quotes off?

Yes. Use the Show quotes switch on the start panel.

Is it comfortable?

Scenes move slowly and you stay in one place the whole time. You can use InnerVerse sitting or standing, and lower the brightness in the menu if you prefer a dimmer view.

Does InnerVerse collect any data?

No. The app cannot even connect to the internet. See the privacy policy.
