Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026 · Anwar Studio Official

The short version. InnerVerse does not collect, transmit or store any personal data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

There is no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising. Everything the app shows and plays is installed on your headset.

1. Who we are

InnerVerse ("the app") is developed by Anwar Studio Official. Questions about this policy or about your privacy go to psopala93@gmail.com, and a real person reads them.

2. What we collect

Nothing. We run no servers for the app and have no database. We receive no data from it: no usage statistics, no crash reports, no IP address and no identifier of any kind. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it is technically unable to connect to any network service.

3. What stays on your headset

The app saves three preferences in its own private storage so that they are remembered next time:

  • the music volume you chose;
  • the scene brightness you chose;
  • whether quotes are shown.

These settings never leave the headset and are removed when you uninstall the app. The app does not read or write any of your own files, photos or videos, and it requests no storage permission.

4. Permissions, in plain terms

PermissionWhat it is for
Hand trackingLets the app respond to your hands as well as the controllers. Hand positions are provided by the headset's system software and are used only in the moment, on the device, to work out what you are pointing at. They are not recorded, stored or transmitted.
OpenXRThe standard system interface a VR app needs to draw to the headset and to read head and controller positions. Used only to render the scene in front of you.

The app requests no internet, storage, microphone, camera, location or contacts permission.

5. Sensors and camera

The app does not access the cameras or the microphone and does not use passthrough, eye tracking, face tracking or body tracking. Head, controller and hand positions are used only to draw the scene and to operate the menu, and are discarded immediately.

6. Third parties

The app contains no third party analytics, advertising or tracking software, and it shares no data with anyone.

7. Children

InnerVerse is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included.

8. The platform

InnerVerse is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. Purchases are handled entirely by Meta, and we never see your payment details. Meta's own handling of your data is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy and is outside our control. We receive nothing from Meta beyond aggregate store statistics.

9. Your rights and data deletion

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. To remove the three saved preferences, uninstall the app or clear its data in the headset's settings.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a material change will be noted in the app's release notes. Since we collect nothing, changes should be rare.
